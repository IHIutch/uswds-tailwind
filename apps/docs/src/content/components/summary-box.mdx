## Example

<ComponentPreview title="Summary Box" code={`
  <div role="complementary" aria-labelledby="summary-box-heading" class="border border-cyan-20 bg-cyan-5 rounded-sm p-6">
    <div id="summary-box-heading" class="text-xl font-bold">Key information</div>
    <div class="mt-4 prose">
      <ul>
        <li>If you are under 18, you cannot apply.</li>
        <li>You must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.</li>
        <li>You must have a valid Social Security number.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
`} />

## Accessibility

- The summary box is a complementary landmark. Its visible heading labels the landmark through `aria-labelledby`.
- Use a heading element inside the heading slot if the title should appear in the page's heading structure.
